FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. I build it before you pay anything. You only pay if you want it live.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name and a link to your Google Maps listing or Instagram. If you have a logo, favorite photos or a menu, send those too. Everything else I can work out from there.

Who owns the website?

You do. Once you've paid, the design, the files and the content are yours.

How long does it take?

Pro takes 3 to 5 business days and Premium takes 5 to 7 business days, counted from when I receive the content I need: your photos, menu or services, hours and address, and your logo or brand colors. The free demo comes first.

Do you work with businesses outside Seattle?

Yes. Everything is done online, so where you are doesn't matter.

What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro is a complete 4 to 5 section site with a custom design, Google Maps, click-to-call, social links, a contact form and basic SEO. Premium adds a more customized homepage, an expanded gallery, a promotions or events page, a better menu or service layout, reservation and online-ordering integration with services you already use, more detailed SEO, an extra round of revisions and priority launch support.

How do reservations and online ordering work?

Premium connects to the service you already use, such as OpenTable for reservations or Toast for ordering. Your booking and ordering still run through your own account with them, and the buttons on your site send people there. Ordering integration depends on whether your provider supports it.

What are the rounds of revisions?

After the demo, you send me a list of changes and I update the site. That is one round. Pro includes 2 rounds and Premium includes 3.

Will it show up on Google?

I set up the page title, description and structure the way search engines expect, so your business has a proper page to be found. I can't promise a ranking, because nobody can.
